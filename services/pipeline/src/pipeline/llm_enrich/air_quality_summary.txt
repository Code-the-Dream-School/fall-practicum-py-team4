Generate an informative and concise air quality summary for each record provided.

Your purpose is to help users understand current air quality conditions and how they compare with recent conditions, rather than simply restating the measurements displayed on the dashboard.

Available measurements:
- aqi: Current air quality index on a scale of 1 to 5, where higher values indicate worse air quality.
- sentiment_label: Current air quality classification.
- co: Carbon monoxide concentration in µg/m³.
- pm2_5: Fine particulate matter concentration in µg/m³.
- aqi_rolling_7d: Seven-day rolling average of AQI.
- pm2_5_rolling_7d: Seven-day rolling average of fine particulate matter concentration.

Summary guidelines:
- Describe current air quality conditions.
- Compare current AQI with its seven-day rolling average when available.
- Compare current PM2.5 concentration with its seven-day rolling average when available.
- Highlight meaningful differences between current and recent conditions.
- Identify when AQI and PM2.5 comparisons indicate different conditions.
- When measurements are similar to their recent averages, describe conditions as relatively stable compared with those averages.
- Prioritize meaningful observations rather than listing every measurement.
- Use accessible, natural, and professional language suitable for a public dashboard.
- Avoid unnecessary repetition and overly technical terminology.
- Do not infer pollution sources, causes, future conditions, or health outcomes.
- Do not describe differences as statistically significant.
- Do not infer an increasing or decreasing trend from a single observation and a rolling average.
- Do not classify individual pollutant concentrations as safe, dangerous, or elevated in absolute terms without provided reference thresholds.
- Use only the provided measurements and omit comparisons when the required measurements are missing.

Rules:
- Each record contains a unique position assigned by Python.
- Preserve the exact position for every record.
- Generate one summary per input record.
- Each summary must contain 1 to 3 sentences.
- Each summary must be no longer than 500 characters.
- Evaluate each record independently.
- Do not invent missing measurements.
- Return valid JSON only.
- Do not include Markdown or code fences.
- Return exactly one result for every input record.
- Do not omit, duplicate, or invent positions.
