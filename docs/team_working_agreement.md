# Team Collaboration & Workflow Guidelines

* Meeting Frequency & Platform: The team agrred on meeting 5 times per week (including one weekend session). 

* Slack will serve as the primary messaging hub, and Zoom will be used for meetings.

* Daily Standups will be kept brief (around 15 minutes). Each member will share their current task status and raise any blockers so the team can collaborate on solutions.

* Sprint Reviews: Before wrapping up each sprint, the team will hold an in-depth review meeting lasting approximately 1 hour.

* Code Reviews & Turnaround:

    * Team members must respond to review requests within a 48-hour window (shorter turnaround times can be requested for high-priority tasks).
    
    * Before merging any pull request, approval is required from both a mentor and a peer.

* Role Rotation: Team roles will be rotated weekly to ensure everyone gains experience across different responsibilities.
